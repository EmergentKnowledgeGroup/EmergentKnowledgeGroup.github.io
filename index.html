Our philosophy

Technology should enhance human relationships, not replace them. So we build the plumbing that makes software treat people well: it remembers from evidence, shows its work, keeps you in charge — and says so when it doesn’t know.

Four habits of everything we build

01 · Evidence

It remembers from evidence.

Memory is only as good as its sources. Our memory and continuity tools keep provenance attached, separate drafts from reviewed truth, and hand work to the next session without losing the thread.

MNOContext Checkpoint

02 · Receipts

It shows its work.

Every important outcome should leave something you can open: a receipt, a ledger entry, a manifest, a report. Not merely a chat claim.

CarsinOSPlotkeeperUPSKAYLEDD

03 · Ownership

You stay in charge.

Local-first where it can be, read-only where it counts. Tools that stay inside what you chose to share, and that spell out what a dangerous action will do before it happens.

CarsinOSCodex AuditLo-fi Defragger

04 · Candor

It says what it doesn’t know.

Abstain when the evidence is thin. Say what isn’t claimed or isn’t ready. Prefer a truthful preview to a flashy promise.
